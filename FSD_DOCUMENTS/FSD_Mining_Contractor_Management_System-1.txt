FUNCTIONAL SPECIFICATION DOCUMENT (FSD) MINING CONTRACTOR MANAGEMENT
SYSTEM (MCMS)

Document Version : 1.0 Document Status : Draft Business Area : Mining
Operation – Coal Mining System Type : Web-Based Mining Contractor
Management System Prepared For : Coal Mining Company Date : August 2026

1. DOCUMENT PURPOSE

Dokumen ini mendefinisikan kebutuhan fungsional untuk Mining Contractor
Management System (MCMS), yaitu sistem terintegrasi untuk mengelola
aktivitas penambangan yang dilaksanakan oleh mining contractor.

Sistem dirancang untuk memberikan kontrol kepada Owner atas: - Mining
plan dan target produksi - Aktivitas dan progress kontraktor - Fleet dan
equipment - Manpower - Coal dan overburden production - Hauling - Survey
dan quantity measurement - Fuel consumption - Contractor performance -
Contract utilization - SLA/KPI - Penalty dan incentive - Progress
claim - Billing verification - Cost per unit - Management reporting

Tujuan akhirnya adalah membentuk single source of truth antara Owner,
Mining Operation, Engineering, Survey, Finance, Procurement/Commercial,
dan Contractor.

2. BUSINESS OBJECTIVES

Production Control: Actual production dapat dibandingkan dengan target
secara harian. Contractor Control: Performance setiap contractor dapat
dimonitor. Quantity Assurance: Quantity invoice harus berdasarkan
quantity yang telah diverifikasi. Cost Control: Mining cost dapat
dimonitor sampai level contractor/pit/activity. Contract Control: Nilai
kontrak, utilization, dan remaining contract dapat dimonitor. Equipment
Control: Productivity dan utilization equipment dapat dianalisis. Fuel
Control: Fuel consumption dapat dibandingkan dengan production output.
Operational Visibility: Management mendapatkan informasi operasional
secara near real-time. Billing Accuracy: Mengurangi dispute dan
overbilling contractor. Performance Management: KPI, penalty, incentive,
dan SLA dihitung secara sistematis. Auditability: Seluruh transaksi
memiliki approval dan audit trail.

3. SYSTEM SCOPE

MCMS mencakup proses Contract → Planning → Operation → Measurement →
Reconciliation → Performance → Billing → Cost → Management Insight.

Functional domain: 1. Master Data Management 2. Contractor & Contract
Management 3. Mining Planning 4. Daily Operation 5. Equipment & Fleet
Management 6. Coal Production 7. Overburden Management 8. Hauling
Management 9. Survey & Measurement 10. Fuel Management 11. Contractor
Performance Management 12. Contract Progress & Utilization 13. Progress
Claim 14. Billing Verification 15. Penalty & Incentive 16. Cost
Management 17. HSE Performance 18. Dashboard & Analytics 19. Workflow &
Approval 20. Document Management 21. Integration 22. Audit Trail &
Security

4. HIGH-LEVEL BUSINESS PROCESS

CONTRACT & WORK ORDER ↓ MINING PLAN ↓ MONTHLY / WEEKLY / DAILY TARGET ↓
CONTRACTOR RESOURCE PLAN ↓ EQUIPMENT + MANPOWER ↓ DAILY MINING OPERATION
↓ OB Removal / Coal Getting / Coal Hauling / Road & Supporting
Activities ↓ SURVEY / MEASUREMENT ↓ PRODUCTION RECONCILIATION ↓
CONTRACTOR PERFORMANCE ↓ QUANTITY VERIFICATION ↓ PROGRESS CLAIM ↓
COMMERCIAL VERIFICATION ↓ PENALTY / INCENTIVE ↓ BILLING ↓ COST ANALYSIS
↓ MANAGEMENT DASHBOARD

5. USER & ROLE MANAGEMENT

Roles: System Administrator, Mining Operation, Mine Engineering,
Surveyor, Dispatch, Fuel Controller, HSE, Contractor User, Contractor
Supervisor, Owner Supervisor, Commercial, Procurement, Finance, Mine
Manager, General Manager, Director, Auditor.

Access control harus mendukung Role-Based Access Control, menu-level
authorization, function-level authorization, site-level access,
contractor-level access, approval authorization, data confidentiality,
serta Read/Create/Update/Approve/Cancel privileges.

Contractor hanya dapat mengakses data yang terkait dengan
perusahaan/kontraknya sendiri.

6. MASTER DATA MANAGEMENT

Organization: Company, Site, Mining Area, Pit, Block, Seam, Stockpile,
Disposal, Hauling Route, Workshop, Fuel Station.

Contractor Master: Contractor ID, Contractor Name, Vendor ID, Address,
Contact Person, Contract Number, Contract Start, Contract End, Status,
Operational Area, PIC Owner, PIC Contractor.

Equipment Master: Equipment ID, Contractor, Equipment Category,
Equipment Type, Brand, Model, Serial Number, Capacity, Ownership,
Commission Date, Status.

Equipment Category: Excavator, Dump Truck, Dozer, Grader, Compactor,
Water Truck, Fuel Truck, Loader, Support Equipment.

Activity Master: OB Removal, Topsoil Removal, Coal Getting, Coal
Hauling, OB Hauling, Road Maintenance, Dewatering, Land Clearing,
Rehabilitation, Supporting Work.

7. CONTRACT MANAGEMENT

Contract Header: Contract Number, Contractor, Contract Type, Effective
Date, Expiry Date, Contract Value, Currency, Site, Scope of Work,
Contract Owner, Payment Terms, Retention, Status.

Contract Rate dapat memiliki multiple rate berdasarkan Activity, Unit,
Effective Date, Distance Range, Material, Equipment, Location,
Escalation Formula, Fuel Adjustment, Minimum Quantity, dan Maximum
Quantity.

Contoh rate: OB Removal (Rp/BCM), Coal Getting (Rp/Ton), Coal Hauling
(Rp/Ton-KM), Equipment Rental (Rp/Hour), Road Maintenance (Rp/Month).

Contract Amendment harus mendukung rate revision, contract extension,
scope addition/reduction, dan contract value adjustment. Semua perubahan
harus mempertahankan historical version.

8. MINING PLAN

Planning hierarchy: Life of Mine → Annual Plan → Monthly Plan → Weekly
Plan → Daily Plan.

Production Target dapat didefinisikan berdasarkan Site, Pit, Contractor,
Activity, Material, dan Period.

KPI: OB Target, Coal Target, Stripping Ratio, Hauling Target, Equipment
Productivity, Fuel Ratio.

9. DAILY OPERATION

Contractor melakukan input Daily Production Report.

Informasi minimum: Date, Shift, Contractor, Pit, Block, Activity,
Material, Loading Point, Dumping Point, Equipment, Quantity, Distance,
Working Hour, Standby Hour, Breakdown Hour.

Workflow: Contractor Input → Contractor Supervisor → Owner Supervisor →
Verified Production.

Setelah verified, data menjadi dasar reconciliation.

10. EQUIPMENT & FLEET MANAGEMENT

Equipment Status: Working, Standby, Breakdown, Maintenance, Waiting,
Idle.

Equipment Hours: Available Hours, Working Hours, Standby Hours,
Breakdown Hours, Maintenance Hours.

KPI: Physical Availability (PA) = Available Hours / Scheduled Hours ×
100% Utilization (UA) = Working Hours / Available Hours × 100% Excavator
Productivity = BCM / Working Hour Hauling Productivity = Ton / Truck
Hour

11. OVERBURDEN MANAGEMENT

System mencatat Pit, Block, Loading Point, Disposal, Equipment,
Contractor, Distance, BCM, dan Working Hour.

Dashboard: OB Plan, OB Actual, Achievement %, BCM/hour, BCM/equipment,
Cost/BCM, Contractor ranking.

12. COAL PRODUCTION

Coal production flow: Coal Getting → ROM → Coal Hauling → Stockpile →
Crusher / Processing.

Data: Seam, Pit, Contractor, Quantity, Quality, Destination, Equipment,
Shift.

System dapat melakukan reconciliation antara Mining Quantity, Survey
Quantity, Weighbridge Quantity, dan Stockpile Quantity.

13. HAULING MANAGEMENT

System menyimpan Origin, Destination, Route, Distance, Material,
Equipment, Trip, Quantity.

Commercial quantity dapat menggunakan Ton × Distance atau BCM ×
Distance, tergantung kontrak.

14. SURVEY & MEASUREMENT

Survey merupakan fungsi critical karena menjadi dasar commercial
quantity.

Data: Survey Date, Survey Period, Pit, Block, Contractor, Material,
Beginning Volume, Ending Volume, Difference, Survey Quantity.

Workflow: Survey Measurement → Survey Verification → Engineering Review
→ Operation Confirmation → Final Quantity.

Final quantity dikunci setelah approval.

15. PRODUCTION RECONCILIATION

System membandingkan Contractor DPR, Owner Operation, Survey, dan
Commercial Approved Quantity.

System menghitung variance secara otomatis. Tolerance dapat
dikonfigurasi, misalnya ±2%.

Jika melebihi tolerance: Exception → Investigation → Resolution →
Approval.

16. FUEL MANAGEMENT

Fuel Transaction: Date, Equipment, Contractor, Fuel Station, Liter, Hour
Meter, Operator, Fuel Truck, Transaction Reference.

Fuel KPI: Fuel Ratio = Liter / BCM Fuel Ratio = Liter / Ton Fuel
Efficiency = Liter / Operating Hour

System membandingkan Standard Fuel Ratio vs Actual Fuel Ratio dan
menghasilkan alert jika melewati threshold.

17. CONTRACTOR PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT

Contractor Scorecard dapat menggunakan kategori dan bobot contoh:
Production 30%, Equipment 15%, Productivity 15%, Fuel 10%, HSE 15%,
Quality 5%, Compliance 10%.

Final Score = Σ (KPI Achievement × KPI Weight)

Output: Contractor Ranking, Monthly Score, Trend, KPI Detail,
Underperformance Alert.

18. HSE PERFORMANCE

Data: Fatality, LTI, Medical Treatment, First Aid, Near Miss, Property
Damage, Environmental Incident, Safety Observation, Inspection, Safety
Compliance.

KPI: LTIFR, TRIFR, Incident Rate, Safety Compliance, Inspection
Compliance.

HSE score dapat menjadi bagian dari contractor performance.

19. CONTRACT PROGRESS

Original Contract Value + Amendment = Revised Contract Value.

Revised Contract Value - Previous Claims - Current Claim = Remaining
Contract.

Dashboard: Contract Value, Utilized Value, Remaining Value, Utilization
%, Contract Expiry, Forecast Completion.

Alert: Contract >80% utilized, Contract >90% utilized, Contract
expiring, Quantity exceeding contract.

20. PROGRESS CLAIM

Contractor dapat membuat progress claim berdasarkan quantity yang telah
approved.

System mengambil otomatis Approved Quantity, Contract Rate, Distance,
Adjustment, Penalty, Incentive.

Gross Claim = Approved Quantity × Contract Rate. Net Claim = Gross
Claim + Incentive - Penalty - Deduction.

21. BILLING VERIFICATION

Workflow: Contractor Claim → Operation Verification → Survey
Verification → Engineering Verification → Commercial Verification → Mine
Manager Approval → Finance → Invoice → Payment.

System harus mencegah invoice apabila quantity belum approved.

22. PENALTY & INCENTIVE

Rule dapat dikonfigurasi berdasarkan contract.

Penalty: Production below target, Equipment availability below SLA,
Excessive fuel consumption, Safety violation, Quality deviation,
Environmental violation.

Incentive: Production above target, Productivity improvement, Fuel
efficiency, Zero incident, Cost reduction.

Calculation engine harus configurable tanpa perubahan source code.

23. COST MANAGEMENT

System menghitung cost berdasarkan Contractor, Pit, Activity, Equipment,
dan Period.

KPI: OB Cost = Total OB Cost / BCM Coal Mining Cost = Mining Cost / Ton
Hauling Cost = Hauling Cost / Ton

Management dapat melihat Budget → Forecast → Actual → Variance.

24. MANAGEMENT DASHBOARD

Executive Dashboard minimal menampilkan: Production: Coal Plan, Coal
Actual, Achievement, OB Plan, OB Actual, Stripping Ratio. Contractor:
Contractor Ranking, Production Achievement, Equipment Availability,
Productivity, Fuel Efficiency, HSE Score. Cost: Mining Cost/Ton, OB
Cost/BCM, Hauling Cost/Ton, Budget vs Actual. Contract: Contract Value,
Utilization, Remaining Value, Expiring Contract. Exception: Production
below target, Fuel anomaly, Equipment underperformance, Quantity
discrepancy, Contract over-utilization, Safety incident.

25. OPERATION CONTROL TOWER

System disarankan memiliki Mining Operation Control Tower yang
menampilkan ringkasan Coal Production, Overburden, Contractor
Performance, Equipment Status, serta operational alerts dalam satu layar
untuk management.

26. ALERT & NOTIFICATION

Configurable alert: - Production <90% target: Production Warning - PA
<85%: Equipment Alert - Fuel > standard +10%: Fuel Alert - Contract
utilization >80%: Contract Warning - Contract expiry <90 days: Contract
Expiry - Survey variance >2%: Quantity Alert - HSE incident: Critical
HSE Alert

Notification melalui in-system notification, email, API/integration, dan
mobile notification pada fase berikutnya.

27. DOCUMENT MANAGEMENT

Dokumen dapat dilampirkan pada Contract, Amendment, Daily Report, Survey
Report, Production Reconciliation, Progress Claim, Invoice, HSE Report,
dan Supporting Evidence.

System menyimpan Document Type, File, Version, Uploaded By, Uploaded
Date, Reference Transaction.

28. WORKFLOW ENGINE

Workflow harus configurable.

Standard status: DRAFT → SUBMITTED → VERIFIED → APPROVED → CLOSED.
Alternatif: REJECTED, RETURN FOR REVISION, CANCELLED.

Setiap approval menyimpan Approver, Date, Decision, Comment, Previous
Status, New Status.

29. AUDIT TRAIL

Setiap transaksi harus mencatat Created By, Created Date, Modified By,
Modified Date, Approved By, Approved Date.

Perubahan critical data harus menyimpan Old Value, New Value, Changed
By, Changed Date, Reason.

Approved transaction tidak boleh diedit langsung. Correction harus
melalui controlled revision/reopen process.

30. INTEGRATION REQUIREMENTS

Architecture harus menyediakan REST API untuk integrasi dengan
ERP/Odoo/SAP, Weighbridge, GPS/Fleet Management System, Fuel Management
System, Laboratory/Coal Quality, HR System, Procurement System, Finance
System, dan Document Management System.

31. DATA IMPORT

Untuk tahap awal implementasi, sistem harus mendukung Excel/CSV import
untuk Production, Equipment, Fuel, Survey, Contract, Rate, Target, HSE,
dan Historical production.

Proses import: Upload → Data Validation → Preview → Error Identification
→ Confirmation → Posting.

32. MOBILE / FIELD OPERATION

System harus responsive untuk Desktop, Tablet, dan Mobile Browser.

Field user harus dapat melakukan Daily activity input, Equipment status
update, Production input, Fuel input, Photo attachment, Approval, dan
Issue reporting.

Future enhancement dapat menggunakan dedicated mobile application.

33. NON-FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS

Performance: standard transaction response <3 seconds; dashboard <5
seconds; heavy analytics dapat menggunakan asynchronous processing.
Availability target minimum: 99.5%. Security minimum: HTTPS, password
encryption, RBAC, session timeout, audit log, API authentication, file
access authorization, database backup. Scalability: multiple sites,
multiple contractors, multiple contracts, millions of production
transactions, historical analytics.

34. CORE DATA MODEL

Core entities: COMPANY, SITE, PIT, BLOCK, SEAM, CONTRACTOR, CONTRACT,
CONTRACT_RATE, EQUIPMENT, EQUIPMENT_STATUS, MINING_PLAN,
PRODUCTION_TARGET, DAILY_OPERATION, PRODUCTION, HAULING,
FUEL_TRANSACTION, SURVEY, RECONCILIATION, CONTRACTOR_KPI,
CONTRACTOR_SCORE, PROGRESS_CLAIM, CLAIM_DETAIL, PENALTY, INCENTIVE,
INVOICE, HSE_INCIDENT, DOCUMENT, WORKFLOW, APPROVAL, USER, ROLE,
AUDIT_LOG.

Relationship utama: CONTRACTOR → CONTRACT → CONTRACT_RATE; CONTRACTOR →
EQUIPMENT; CONTRACTOR → PRODUCTION; CONTRACTOR → FUEL; CONTRACTOR → KPI;
CONTRACTOR → PROGRESS_CLAIM.

35. KEY BUSINESS CONTROLS

BR-001 Production quantity yang belum diverifikasi tidak boleh digunakan
untuk billing. BR-002 Survey quantity yang telah final tidak dapat
diubah tanpa reopening approval. BR-003 Rate billing harus berasal dari
active contract rate. BR-004 Contractor tidak dapat mengakses transaksi
contractor lain. BR-005 Invoice tidak dapat melebihi approved progress
claim. BR-006 Claim tidak dapat melebihi remaining contract
quantity/value tanpa approval. BR-007 Duplicate production transaction
harus dicegah. BR-008 Fuel transaction harus memiliki equipment valid.
BR-009 Equipment harus terasosiasi dengan contractor yang valid pada
periode transaksi. BR-010 Penalty dan incentive harus mempunyai
calculation evidence. BR-011 Approved transaction harus immutable dan
perubahan dilakukan melalui controlled revision.

36. IMPLEMENTATION PHASING

Phase 1 – Mining Operation Control: Master Data, Contractor, Contract,
Mining Plan, Daily Production, Equipment, Survey, Reconciliation,
Dashboard. Outcome: single source of truth untuk monitoring produksi
contractor.

Phase 2 – Contractor Performance & Cost: Fuel, Equipment KPI, Contractor
KPI, HSE, Cost Analysis, Penalty & Incentive. Outcome: mengukur
produktivitas dan efisiensi contractor.

Phase 3 – Commercial & Billing: Contract utilization, Progress claim,
Commercial verification, Billing, ERP integration. Outcome: Production →
Measurement → Claim → Invoice terintegrasi.

Phase 4 – Intelligent Mining Management: Production forecasting, Fuel
anomaly detection, Equipment productivity analytics, Contractor
performance prediction, Cost forecasting, Automated management insights,
Natural-language analytics.

37. TARGET END-STATE

Target platform mengintegrasikan Management/Executive Dashboard, Mining
Control Tower, Planning, Operation, Commercial, Survey/Reconciliation,
Contractor KPI, Cost Analytics, dan Insight Engine.

MCMS diposisikan sebagai Owner Mining Management Platform yang
menghubungkan operational, technical, contractor performance,
commercial, dan financial dalam satu data model.
